Rear deltoid pain can have several possible causes:

## Common Causes

**Muscle Strain or Overuse**
- Repetitive overhead movements or pulling exercises
- Poor form during exercises like rows, reverse flys, or face pulls
- Sudden increase in training intensity or volume
- Sports involving throwing or swimming

**Poor Posture**
- Rounded shoulders and forward head posture can stress the rear deltoids
- Prolonged sitting or computer work leading to muscle imbalances

**Rotator Cuff Issues**
- Inflammation or tears in the rotator cuff muscles
- The rear deltoid works closely with these muscles, so problems can cause referred pain

**Muscle Imbalances**
- Overdeveloped front deltoids and chest muscles relative to rear deltoids
- Weak upper back muscles forcing rear deltoids to compensate

**Tendinitis**
- Inflammation of the deltoid tendon from repetitive stress
- Can develop gradually over time

**Shoulder Impingement**
- Compression of tendons or bursa in the shoulder joint
- May cause pain that radiates to the rear deltoid area

**Trigger Points**
- Tight knots in the muscle tissue causing localized pain
- Can develop from stress, overuse, or poor posture

## When to Seek Medical Attention

See a healthcare provider if you experience:
- Severe or persistent pain
- Significant weakness or loss of range of motion
- Pain that doesn't improve with rest
- Swelling, bruising, or deformity
- Pain following an injury or trauma

**Initial Treatment**: Rest, ice, anti-inflammatory medication, gentle stretching, and avoiding aggravating activities often help. If pain persists, a proper evaluation by a healthcare professional can determine the specific cause and appropriate treatment plan.
