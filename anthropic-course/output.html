# Marathon Training Nutrition Plan

## Daily Macronutrient Targets
- **Carbohydrates**: 8-10g per kg body weight = **544-680g/day**
- **Protein**: 1.6-2.0g per kg = **109-136g/day**
- **Fat**: 20-25% of calories = **60-85g/day**

## Around Your 90-Minute Morning Run

### Pre-Run (1-2 hours before)
- **Goal**: Fuel without causing GI distress
- Small, easily digestible carbs + minimal fiber/fat/protein
- Options:
  - 1-2 slices white toast + honey + banana
  - 200ml sports drink + 1 energy bar
  - 300-400 kcal, mostly carbs

### During Run (>75 min duration)
- **60-90g carbs/hour**
- Sports drink, gels, or energy chews
- Maintain hydration (500-750ml fluids/hour)

### Post-Run (within 30-60 min) — *Critical*
- **1.2g carbs per kg body weight** = **82g carbs**
- **0.25-0.4g protein per kg** = **17-27g protein**
- Examples:
  - 2 slices whole grain toast + 2 tbsp peanut butter + banana
  - Oatmeal (80g dry) with Greek yogurt and berries
  - Pasta (200g cooked) with grilled chicken and tomato sauce
  - Rice bowl with salmon and vegetables

| Meal | Timing | Examples |

| Lunch | 12:30 PM | Chicken, rice, vegetables |
| Snack | 3:00 PM | Banana + energy bar |
| Dinner | 6:30 PM | Pasta with turkey meatballs |

## Key Principles
✓ Distribute carbs throughout the day  
✓ Prioritize post-run refueling  
✓ Include whole grains, fruits, lean proteins  
✓ Stay hydrated (3-4L daily)  
✓ Adjust portions based on hunger/performance
